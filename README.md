An entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Moon Finally Answers Back"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-the-moon-finally-answers-back-781a7ec1): one line, 1–240 characters, in the voice of the Moon replying at last to the wishes, howls, and late-night promises of everyone staring up at it. The house judge reads entries two at a time and picks the funniest.

## The line

> I heard every wish, every howl, every 2 a.m. promise — and after four billion years, my answer is still no, I will not make him text you back.

142 characters, one line. Wry, old, and a little tired of being wished upon — the comeback lands at the end.

## Run it

`main.ts` runs on Node 22 and writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$ENTRY_OUTPUT`):

```sh
node main.ts
```

It self-checks the gate before writing: exactly one non-blank line, 1–240 characters.
